# Care for common plants, kept by hand. care.cs in this folder turns it into
# src/Stikling.Web/wwwroot/data/plant-care.json, which the plant form and plant page offer from.
#
# One plant to a line:
#
#   Genus species: light | water | humidity | season | rest | cats | dogs | people | care source | pets source | people source
#
# A line with only a genus counts for every plant in that genus that has no line of its own. A
# plant with a line of its own gets nothing from the genus line.
#
# Values, or ? when no source says:
#   light     low, medium, bright-indirect, direct-sun (the light it likes, indoors)
#   water     dry-out, dry-partly, moist
#   humidity  low, medium, high
#   season    summer (rests in winter), winter (rests in summer), all-year
#   rest      cooler, drier, cooler-drier, dies-back (only with summer or winter)
#   cats, dogs, people   toxic, non-toxic
#
# Every value comes from the source on its line, and a source is ? when its values are all ?. Light,
# water and humidity are only taken from what a source says about growing the plant indoors. Ratings
# meant for a garden, like "full sun" or "part shade, 2 to 6 hours of direct sun", don't count. A
# season is only set when the source says the plant goes dormant, not when it only says to water
# less in winter. Cats and dogs come from the ASPCA. People comes from NC State Extension's poison
# section, and only when that section is about eating the leaves or stems.
#
# How the words on a page were read:
#   light     "Bright indirect", "bright filtered", "filtered sun" and "bright light with no direct
#             sun" are bright-indirect. "Medium", "moderate" and "indirect or low" light are medium.
#             "Sunny window" and "some direct sun" are direct-sun.
#   water     "Let it dry out between waterings" is dry-out. "Top inch dry", "almost dry on top",
#             "dry slightly" and "never let it dry out completely" are dry-partly. "Keep evenly
#             moist" and "do not let it dry out" are moist. "Water regularly" alone is ?.
#   humidity  "High humidity", 50 to 60% or more, or a humidifier or pebble tray is high. "Medium"
#             or "moderate" is medium. "Tolerates dry air" or "home humidity is fine" is low.
#
# Use the name the plant goes by now, as in plant-names.json. Old names find the line anyway, since
# care.cs adds them. The build warns about a name it doesn't know.
